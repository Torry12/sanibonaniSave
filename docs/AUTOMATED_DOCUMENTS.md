# Automated and Downloadable Documents

SanibonaniSave provides dynamically generated documents tailored to each group type. These documents are generated as PDFs and are mandatory prerequisites for "Full Membership" status.

## Document Types

### 1. Group Constitution
The Group Constitution defines the operational rules, governance, and financial policies of the group. It is generated during group registration or can be regenerated by administrators.

#### Tailored Content by Group Type
| Group Type | Key Specific Sections |
|------------|-----------------------|
| **Burial Society** | Waiting periods (Accidental vs Natural death), Claim submission procedures, Beneficiary age limits, and Benefit tiers. |
| **Stokvel** | Savings cycle duration, Annual payout distribution rules, and share-based dividend calculations. |
| **ROSCA** | Detailed rotation schedule, "Pot" calculation logic, and strict non-withdrawal commitment during the cycle. |
| **Investment Club** | Net Asset Value (NAV) valuation methodology, Unitization rules, Investment Committee roles, and risk disclosures. |
| **Emergency Fund** | Definition of qualifying "Emergencies", vetting process for requests, and liquidity reserve requirements (e.g., 50% cap). |

---

### 2. Membership Contract
The Membership Contract is a legally binding agreement between the individual member and the group. It must be downloaded, signed, and re-uploaded by the member.

#### Mandatory Fields
- Member Full Name and ID Number
- Group Name and Type
- Monthly Contribution Amount
- Payment Due Date (e.g., "by the 7th of each month")

#### Tailored Clauses by Group Type
| Group Type | Specific Clause |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Burial Society** | "The member acknowledges a 6-month waiting period for natural causes and understands that benefits are only paid for registered beneficiaries." |
| **Stokvel** | "The member agrees to the annual distribution cycle and understands that early withdrawal may result in forfeiture of dividends." |
| **ROSCA** | "The member commits to the full rotation cycle and understands they are obligated to contribute until the final member has received the pot." |
| **Investment Club** | "The member acknowledges that capital is at risk and returns are not guaranteed, fluctuating with the group's Net Asset Value (NAV)." |
| **Emergency Fund** | "The member understands that funds are for emergency use only and withdrawals are subject to committee approval and liquidity caps." |

---

## Workflow and Prerequisites

1. **Registration**: Upon joining or creating a group, the documents are automatically generated.
2. **Download**: Members/Admins download the tailored PDFs from their dashboard.
3. **Execution**: The document is signed (physical signature or digital acceptance).
4. **Upload**: The signed document is uploaded to the "Documents" tab.
5. **Verification**: An administrator (or the platform) verifies the document, writing `verified` / `rejected` to `members.document_N_status` and `contract_status` — lowercase in Postgres, `DocumentStatus` in Kotlin.
6. **Full Membership**: Verification does **not** promote the member. Promotion off `probation` is time-based: `ProbationCompletionWorker` compares `probation_end_at` to now and requires the joining fee to be `paid` (type `joining_fee` or the legacy `registration_contribution`) before it posts `MemberStatus.ACTIVE`.

> [!WARNING]
> `ProbationCompletionWorker` is one of the six `app/…/worker` classes that no code path enqueues, so today the promotion only happens when an admin changes status explicitly. The same is true of the document gate: nothing in `PayoutGuard`, `ValidateLoanEligibilityUseCase` or the voting RLS policy refuses an unverified contract — `LoansTabSections.kt:227` only shows the admin a `document1Status == VERIFIED || idNumber is set` hint. The one real consequence lives in `VerifyMemberDocumentUseCase`: rejecting a document immediately suspends the member, while verifying one changes nothing beyond the document column.
