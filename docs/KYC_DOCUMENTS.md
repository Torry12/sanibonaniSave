# 📄 SanibonaniSave — KYC & Membership Prerequisites

This document outlines the mandatory documentation required for members and groups to achieve "Full Membership" status. These documents are dynamically generated based on the group type.

---

## 🏗️ 1. Group-Level Prerequisites

Before a group can accept contributions or approve payouts, the following must be verified by the Platform Admin:

| Document | Description | Target Group |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Constitution** | Tailored rules covering governance, fees, and payouts. | ALL |
| **Burial Policy Annexure**| Actuarial waiting periods and claim requirements. | Burial Society |
| **Rotation Schedule** | Fixed rotation order and participant cycle agreement. | ROSCA |

---

## 👤 2. Member-Level Prerequisites

Members are in `PROBATION` until these documents are uploaded and verified:

### Common Requirements (All Groups)
1.  **Identity Document**: South African Green ID or Smart Card.
2.  **Proof of Residence**: Utility bill or municipal letter (not older than 3 months).
3.  **Membership Contract**: Signed agreement to abide by group rules and platform fees.

### Specialized Requirements
| Group Type | Additional Document | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Burial Society** | **Beneficiary ID Copies** | Required for actuarial risk assessment. |
| **Investment Club** | **Equity Acknowledgment** | Agreement on NAV fluctuating unit prices. |
| **ROSCA** | **Cycle Acknowledgment** | Agreement to stay for the full 3/6/12 month cycle. |

---

## 📥 3. Automated Document Generation

The app automatically generates PDF templates for the following:

1.  **Membership Contract**: Pre-filled with member name, ID, group name, and monthly contribution amount.
2.  **Group Constitution**: Pre-filled with group name, city, joining fee, and specialized logic (e.g., Stokvel payout dates).

### Download & Upload Flow
1.  Member registers for a group.
2.  In **Documents Tab**, member clicks "Download Membership Contract".
3.  Member signs the document (physically or digitally).
4.  Member uploads the signed copy using the **Upload** button.
5.  Group Admin verifies the document, moving member from `PROBATION` to `ACTIVE`.

---

## ⚖️ 4. Legal Compliance

All generated documents comply with the **Co-operatives Act of South Africa** and follow best practices for Stokvel associations (e.g., NASASA standards).
